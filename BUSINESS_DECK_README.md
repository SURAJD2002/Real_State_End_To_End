## 1. Executive Positioning & Target Audience

### Core Positioning
> **Planwise Enterprise**
> *"Land-to-Home Development & Construction Platform"*
> **From a piece of land to a build-ready project.**

Planwise is positioned as a **computational real-estate development platform** connecting statutory land feasibility, computational house design, model-linked material quantities, regional cost waterfalls, and licensed professional review into one traceable, deterministic workflow.

It is **not** positioned as an AI floor-plan generator, a concept renderer, or a generic construction ERP. It is an end-to-end computational graph where physical site boundaries directly drive municipal setbacks, generative room topology, semantic 3D models (CBM), itemized BOQs, regional cost waterfalls, and engineer sign-off gates.

### Target Audience
- **Real-Estate Developers & Land Promoters:** Seeking rapid statutory screening, lower pre-construction carry costs, and elimination of downstream change orders.
- **Residential Builders & Plotting Firms:** Requiring standardized project models, reliable initial cost envelopes, and transparent client workflows.
- **Architectural Practices:** Looking to automate tedious municipal zoning and schematic drafting while maintaining bespoke creative control.
- **Structural & MEP Engineers:** Seeking pre-coordinated 3D models with verified site boundaries and explicit, paid review gates.
- **General Contractors:** Requiring verified 7-trade BOQs tied directly to 3D element IDs to prevent quantity disputes.
- **Real-Estate Technology Partners (GIS, BIM, Proptech):** Exploring headless API licensing for automated feasibility and zoning calculations.
- **Strategic Investors & Incubators:** Seeking defensible deep-tech platforms modernizing the multi-trillion-dollar residential development industry.
